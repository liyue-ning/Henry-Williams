EVALUATION

In terms of the early spread of the Gospel in Aotearoa New Zealand, I believe that Henry Williams was a significant individual. Though I did not research nearly as much about other missionaries, I think that Williams was significant due to his different approach to Sammuel Marson which overtime, was successful and arguably more effective. Furthermore, the fact that he was influential is evidence of his significance as it shows how the Māori saw him as a peacemaker—reflecting patience and forgiveness of God—and how he was able to contribute to the translation of the Treaty of Waitangi which is important because Williams was able to introduce the idea of a covenant—a binding, agreement, relationship established between humanity and God.

Without Henry Williams, I'd argue that a lot less natives would have been baptized and learnt the word of God due to his method of spiritual teachings instead of Sammuel's method. As a result, by prioritizing the spread of the Gospel and learning about the word, the natives would be able to spread it themselves at an earlier rate. His significance also is highlighted through other actions such as independently baptizing more than 1000 Māori by 1844. Which I think is incredibly impressive for a single individual, because not only may a religion be new and unfamiliar to them, but Williams also had to be patience and have themselves choose God independently, resulting in a significant amount of effort and time spent with the natives.

The early spread of the Gospel impacted the community because it introduced literacy as the Early Church Missionary Society (CMS) developed a written alphabet and dictionary for the Māori as well as introducing the printing press, which allowed the New Testament to be spread at a quicker rate. Moreover, the Gospel was a tool for peace evident in how the Christian message of forgiveness was used to eventually end the Musket Wars (it lasted for decades). In addition, the early spread of the Gospel lead to the freeing of slaves, which means that if they had not been present, it would have been likely these positive outcomes would have occurred at a much later rate.
